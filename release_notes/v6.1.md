**Version 6.1** of the file that lets Budget Lock's assistant understand what you type.

- **Size:** 478 MB
- **Works with:** Budget Lock 1.8 or newer on Android, and Budget Lock for Windows
- **What changed:** "set up a goal" and "set up a budget" are understood in every one of the app's 50 languages, and the assistant no longer writes English notes or names into entries made in another language
- **Add it:** the assistant is a beta and starts switched off. Tap *Turn it on* when Budget Lock offers it, or turn it on in Settings → Offline Assistant; then open Assistant and tap *Download it here*, or follow the steps at https://budgetlock.app/model

The assistant uses an AI model and sometimes gets things wrong. Check what it did; Undo reverses a change.
